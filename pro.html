Questions

Is the free plan really free?

Yes. News, the calendar, learning, calculators, the Journal and the Terminal desks stay free. You also get a daily allowance of every premium lab, so you can see exactly what you would be paying for.

What happens when I hit the daily limit?

The tool tells you, your allowance resets at midnight, and you can upgrade at any moment to keep going. Nothing you have saved is lost.

How do I cancel?

Open Manage subscription on this page and cancel renewal. Paid access continues until the end of the period you have paid for.

How is my payment handled?

By Stripe. FYNX never sees your card. Your plan is confirmed on our servers from Stripe before any paid tool unlocks.

Can I change plans?

To move between paid plans, cancel renewal in Manage subscription and choose the new plan when the period ends, or contact support and we will help.
