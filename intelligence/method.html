How FYNX Intelligence is made

What we collect

Headlines, publication times, links and short descriptions from publisher RSS feeds and news services that FYNX already uses, plus short source excerpts only where a publisher's site allows automated access. We never bypass paywalls and never store or republish full articles.

How stories are chosen

Reports of the same event are grouped together. A story gets an Intelligence page only when it names a market FYNX can measure — a company with a listed share, an index, a currency, a commodity, a crypto asset or US rates — and has either several independent reports or a usable source excerpt. Stories about allegations, accidents or violence are held back for human review.

Verified data

Prices and daily history come from FYNX's hourly market snapshot (Yahoo Finance, delayed). Treasury yields come from the U.S. Treasury; Fed funds pricing from CBOT futures and the New York Fed; policy rates from the Bank for International Settlements; the economic calendar from FYNX's weekly calendar snapshot; earnings dates from Nasdaq. Every figure on a page names its source and time.

AI-assisted analysis

When enabled, an AI model drafts the overview, interpretation and scenarios from the collected facts only. An automatic check rejects any draft that contains a number, quotation or cited source that is not in those facts; rejected stories are not published. Pages say plainly whether AI was used. AI drafts are not reviewed by a human editor before publication.

Updates and corrections

Developing stories are re-checked on every run for 36 hours: new reports are added as sources and market data is refreshed, with the update time shown on the page. Pages are kept for 21 days. To report an error, use the contact page.

Publishers

Original reporting belongs to its publishers. FYNX credits and links every source and is not affiliated with or endorsed by them.
