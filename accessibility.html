ACCESS & CONNECTION HELP

A simpler way to browse.

Read public lessons and use local calculations without an account. If an embedded service is unavailable, you can still explore the rest of FYNX.

Open the lightweight directory →

If the whole website is blocked

Check that the address is www.fynxfinanceworld.com and uses HTTPS. If your school or organization shows a blocked-category message, share this address and the message with its IT administrator. Access policies are controlled by the organization.

FYNX offers educational finance material and calculation tools. It does not execute brokerage trades. An administrator can review the site and decide whether it fits the network’s policy.

If charts or sign-in do not load

Some features use external services. Firebase handles accounts and cloud data; TradingView supplies selected embedded charts and calendars. A managed network may allow this site while restricting those providers. Public lessons, the directory and local calculators do not require you to sign in.

Use the text links beside embedded content to open a standalone resource or the data publisher. Saved resource shortcuts are kept in this browser; if browser storage is disabled, browsing still works but saves may not persist.

If you see a connection or certificate error

Check the address, your device’s date and time, and whether the network has a sign-in page. Do not ignore a certificate warning. Share the exact error, browser and affected address with your administrator or FYNX support.

Keyboard and low-bandwidth access

Use Tab to move through links and controls, Enter to activate them and Escape to close open menus. The resource library has labeled search and category controls. The lightweight directory works without JavaScript; lesson text and links are delivered in the initial page.
