07 / MARKET HOURS API

Is the market open?
Ask once.

Open, closed or lunch break for seven exchanges, with the next open and close in UTC, published holiday calendars and the four forex sessions.

Regular cash-equity sessions from published exchange schedules. Pre-market, after-hours, auctions and emergency closures are not included.

Four endpoints.

EndpointWhat it returns
statusOpen, closed, break or unknown for NYSE, Nasdaq, LSE, TSX, TSXV, HKEX and TSE, with next open and next close
holidaysHolidays and early closes for one exchange and year, with coverage status
sessionsSydney, Tokyo, London and New York forex sessions, active overlaps and the weekend gap
exchangesSupported exchanges, local trading hours and calendar coverage

WORKED EXAMPLE / 2 NOV 2026, 14:30 UTC

New York opens.
Hong Kong is asleep.

At 14:30 UTC on Monday 2 November 2026, NYSE is open until 21:00 UTC and London is open until 16:30 UTC. Hong Kong is closed and next opens at 01:30 UTC.

Daylight-saving changes, lunch breaks and published holidays are handled for you. Dates outside published calendars return unknown with a warning, never a guess of open.

POST /v1/markets/status
{
  "exchanges": [
    "NYSE",
    "LSE",
    "HKEX"
  ],
  "at": "2026-11-02T14:30:00Z"
}

Seven exchanges.

NYSE, Nasdaq, LSE, TSX, TSXV, HKEX and TSE, including Hong Kong and Tokyo lunch breaks.

Forex sessions.

Active sessions and overlaps (for example London + New York), plus the Sunday-to-Friday forex week.

Any moment.

Pass an at timestamp to check the past or plan ahead; otherwise the current time is used.
