08 / PORTFOLIO API

Know your numbers
before the market does.

Turn closed trades, equity history and open positions into performance, drawdown and exposure metrics your users can trust.

Metrics use the trades, equity points and positions you send. FYNX does not connect to brokers for these routes.

Three endpoints.

EndpointWhat it returnsExample result
performanceWin rate, profit factor, payoff ratio, expectancy, streaks, average R and return5 trades → 40% win rate, profit factor 3, +2% on $10,000
drawdownMaximum and current drawdown, recovery point, longest drawdown; Sharpe, Sortino and volatility when you send periods per year100 → 120 → 90 → 130 → 110: 25% max drawdown, recovered at point 3
exposureGross, net, long and short exposure, leverage, concentration, asset-class totals and forex currency legs$128,000 gross on $50,000 equity: 2.56× leverage

WORKED EXAMPLE / EXPOSURE

One forex lot.
One stock position.

Long 1 lot of EURUSD at 1.08 and 100 AAPL at $200 on a $50,000 account give $128,000 gross exposure and 2.56× leverage. EURUSD is 84.38% of gross.

Forex positions also report currency legs: +100,000 EUR and −108,000 USD, so you can see what you are really long and short.

POST /v1/portfolio/exposure
{
  "account_equity": "50000",
  "account_currency": "USD",
  "positions": [
    {
      "symbol": "EURUSD",
      "side": "long",
      "quantity": "1",
      "contract_size": "100000",
      "price": "1.08",
      "asset_class": "forex"
    },
    {
      "symbol": "AAPL",
      "side": "long",
      "quantity": "100",
      "price": "200",
      "asset_class": "stock"
    }
  ]
}

Up to 1,000 trades.

Performance accepts up to 1,000 closed trades and 2,000 equity points per request.

Honest edge cases.

No losses means profit factor is null with a warning, not infinity. Fewer than 30 returns flags Sharpe and Sortino.

Your currency.

Send a conversion rate for positions not priced in the account currency.
