12 / OPTIONS API PREVIEW

Price any option.
See every greek.

Five endpoints for options traders and the apps they use: Black-Scholes-Merton pricing and greeks, implied volatility, strategy payoff, price probabilities and expected move.

Preview — activation pending. Options endpoints go live with the next API release. The developer workspace offers them as soon as the service is activated.
Results use only the numbers you send: spot, strike, days, volatility, rates. European exercise, continuous dividend yield, time = calendar days / 365. Models, not investment advice.

Five endpoints.

EndpointWhat it returns
POST /v1/options/pricePrice, intrinsic and time value, delta, gamma, theta per day, vega and rho per point, and the probability of finishing in the money. Add contracts for position totals.
POST /v1/options/implied-volatilityThe volatility that reproduces a market price, with greeks at that volatility. Prices outside no-arbitrage bounds return a clear 422.
POST /v1/options/payoffProfit at expiry for up to 8 call, put and stock legs: exact max profit, max loss (or unlimited), break-evens and a payoff table.
POST /v1/options/probabilityFor up to 20 prices: probability of finishing above or below at expiry and of touching before expiry.
POST /v1/options/expected-moveExpected move from implied volatility, with 1 and 2 standard deviation price ranges.

Its own key scope.

Keys need options:calculate. Keys created with a product list keep exactly the products they had; create a new key with Options ticked after activation.

Checked against the textbook.

Prices match the Hull reference example, greeks agree with finite differences, and implied volatility recovers the input to 0.0001 points.

Exact payoffs.

Max profit, max loss and break-evens are solved exactly at each strike, and unlimited upside or risk is reported as null with a flag, never a made-up number.
