QuestionLaunch policy / current status
What counts toward usage?Proposed: successful API operations; failed validation, authentication failures, throttled requests and duplicate idempotent replays are not billable. Operational logs are separate.
What happens at the free limit?Proposed: requests are rejected with a quota explanation until reset or an explicit upgrade. No surprise charges.
What does overage cost?Not finalized. No paid overage can be enabled until a per-call price and spending policy are published and accepted.
When does the allowance reset?Proposed: calendar month at 00:00 UTC on the first day. Final terms will be displayed before activation.
Are test calls billed?Separate test keys are planned. Test calls are unbilled and still subject to abuse-prevention limits.
Is the $49 price in the build brief final?It is the proposed launch base price, confirmed for this preview. Final allowances and overage rates are still pending; it is not an available subscription.

Before you subscribe

Final included calls, rate limits, overage rates, retention terms and support coverage will be published together. Nothing on this preview authorizes a charge.
