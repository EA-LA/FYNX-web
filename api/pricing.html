No guessing about overages.

QuestionLaunch policy / current status
What counts toward usage?Successful API operations; failed validation, authentication failures, throttled requests and duplicate idempotent replays are not billable. Operational logs are separate.
What happens at the free limit?Requests are rejected with a quota explanation until the monthly reset. If you set a lower cap, you can raise it within your allowance. Paid upgrades are not yet available.
What does overage cost?No usage overages are sold in this release. Requests stop at the included allowance or your lower saved cap.
When does the allowance reset?Calendar month at 00:00 UTC on the first day.
Are test calls billed?Test and live keys are separate. Each environment has its own allowance. Test calls are unbilled and subject to request limits.
Is the $49 price in the build brief final?It is the proposed launch base price, confirmed for this preview. It includes 50,000 live calls per month with no automatic overage charges. Live paid activation is pending.

Before you subscribe

Free: 1,000 calls/month per environment. Pro: 50,000 live calls/month at $49/month, with test usage on its separate free allowance. Paid plans are on a waitlist until paid subscriptions are available; no real charge is made.
