QuestionAnswer
What counts toward usage?Successful API operations. Failed validation, authentication failures, throttled requests and duplicate idempotent replays are not counted.
How do extra calls work?Paid plans stop at their included calls by default. To keep serving beyond that, raise your monthly request cap in Settings (up to 10× your plan). Extra calls are billed per started block of 1,000: $0.50 on Developer, $0.30 on Business, added to your next invoice.
Can I limit my spending?Yes. Your saved cap is a hard limit: requests stop when it is reached. Set it at or below your included calls and you never pay for extra calls.
What happens at the Free limit?Requests are rejected with a quota explanation until the monthly reset, or until you choose a paid plan.
When does the allowance reset?On the first of each month at 00:00 UTC. Invoices follow your purchase date.
Are test calls billed?No. Test and live keys are separate; test usage has its own free allowance and is never billed.
I have API Pro at $49. What changes?Nothing. Your 50,000 calls and 30 requests per second continue until you cancel. You can switch to Developer or Business afterwards.

Before you subscribe

Subscriptions renew monthly until canceled. Cancel renewal in Manage billing; paid access continues until the period ends. Extra-call charges for a period appear on the following invoice. Limits are shared across all keys in an environment.
