No guessing about overages.

QuestionLaunch policy / current status
What counts toward usage?Successful API operations; failed validation, authentication failures, throttled requests and duplicate idempotent replays are not billable. Operational logs are separate.
What happens at the free limit?Requests are rejected with a quota explanation until the monthly reset. If you set a lower cap, you can raise it within your allowance. Subscribe to Pro for a 50,000-call live allowance.
What does overage cost?No usage overages are sold in this release. Requests stop at the included allowance or your lower saved cap.
When does the allowance reset?Calendar month at 00:00 UTC on the first day.
Are test calls billed?Test and live keys are separate. Each environment has its own allowance. Test calls are unbilled and subject to request limits.
What does API Pro include?The $49 monthly subscription includes 50,000 live calls per UTC calendar month and up to 30 requests per second, shared across both APIs. No automatic overage charges apply.

Before you subscribe

Free: 1,000 calls/month per environment. Pro: 50,000 live calls/month at $49/month, with test usage on its separate free allowance. Subscriptions renew monthly until canceled. Cancel renewal in Manage billing; paid access continues until the subscription period ends. Invoice dates follow your purchase date; usage resets on the first of each month at 00:00 UTC.
