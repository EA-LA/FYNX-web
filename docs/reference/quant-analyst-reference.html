Every number on this page is computed in your browser from real daily FX closes (Federal Reserve H.10 series via the open datasets/exchange-rates archive), 10 years to the last close. Gold tick data and live news need the data-feed backend described in step 06.

Markets

News events

NFP days → EUR/USD event

NFP days use the first-Friday release rule; a few months the BLS shifts the date, which the production feed fixes with the real calendar.

ATR breakout

Entries and exits are taken at the daily close. ATR is the average absolute close-to-close move (daily closes only). Costs are charged per round trip.

Entry × stop surface

72 backtests: entry threshold 0–2 ATR × stop 0.5–4 ATR. Optimized on the first 70% of history, then checked on the last 30% it never saw.

Portfolio

Eight sleeves: the current ATR breakout settings on seven majors plus the NFP news strategy. Add your own strategies from a CSV of daily returns (date,return_pct).

2,000 Monte Carlo paths

Block bootstrap of the portfolio's real daily returns (10-day blocks keep volatility clusters), one year ahead.

FTMO 10k · 2-step check

Rules modelled: Phase 1 +10% ($1,000), Phase 2 +5% ($500), max daily loss 5% ($500), max loss 10% ($1,000, static), minimum 4 trading days, no time limit (capped here at the horizon below). Confirm current rules on ftmo.com before buying.
