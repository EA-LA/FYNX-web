    How FYNX prepares this page

    FYNX checks the source material available to its news service and prepares a concise reading brief. The source-check panel states whether that material is public article text, a publisher excerpt, or only a headline. Missing reporting is never filled with invented facts.

    FYNX context explains general financial and economic mechanisms; it is separate from the source’s claims. Publication time, preparation time and the most recent check are shown separately. Source images retain their attribution; context images are explicitly labeled and do not claim to depict the event.

    This page checks for updates every five minutes while visible. It preserves your reading position and lets you apply an updated brief when one is available. Original reporting and publisher access terms remain at the source.
