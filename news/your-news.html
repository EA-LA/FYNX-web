How Your News works

Your chosen topics and edition determine the feeds requested. Top reporting follows the source feed's ranking; Newest first uses publication time. Current feeds contain reports from the past 24 hours and check for updates every five minutes while this page is visible. They also refresh when you change topics or edition.

Unread means you have not opened that report in this browser's recorded history. Your preferences stay in this browser. Up to 60 recently opened briefs remain in its history for up to seven days; they do not sync to an account. Clear history removes the local reading list. Your existing research saves and membership remain separate.

Coverage can be empty or partially refreshed when a source is unavailable. FYNX does not fill those gaps with invented reporting. Publication times, source names and available evidence appear with the reporting.
