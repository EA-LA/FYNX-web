Translate a thesis into a testable exposure

A macro thesis is a claim about an economic mechanism. Turning it into a paper trade requires an instrument, time horizon and a reason that the view is not already fully reflected in price. Several instruments can express a similar idea but have different carry, liquidity, leverage and event risks.

Write the ways the thesis can fail

Separate economic invalidation from a price-based exit and a time limit. A thesis can be directionally right but lose money because of entry price, timing or costs. Review the observed evidence after the event and record what would change your view. A persuasive story is not a substitute for a measurable position plan.

WORKED EXAMPLE

Put the idea into numbers

A view that inflation will ease does not uniquely determine a bond, equity or currency position. Each expression also depends on growth, expectations, valuation and the horizon over which the view plays out.

Illustrative assumptions; no live quote or promised outcome.

Try it in your research

Write one macro hypothesis and two hypothetical instruments that could express it. Compare their carry, risk drivers and falsification conditions before selecting a paper example.
