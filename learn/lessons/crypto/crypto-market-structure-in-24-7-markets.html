How trend, chop, and weekend liquidity differ from stocks.

A continuous market still has interruptions

Crypto trading runs across many venues around the clock, but liquidity and access are not constant. Exchanges can pause trading or withdrawals, stablecoins can deviate from their reference value, and an instrument’s price can differ across venues. A chart should identify the exchange and the quote asset.

Observe liquidity at the intended size

The spread and order-book depth matter more than a headline volume number when estimating an execution. Weekend conditions can differ from weekday conditions, but the effect varies by asset and venue. Record depth, spread and realized movement together. Large price changes can occur when fewer orders are available to absorb a trade.

WORKED EXAMPLE

Put the idea into numbers

A token quoted at 10.00 on one venue and 10.15 on another does not establish a risk-free opportunity. Fees, withdrawal restrictions, transfer time and available depth can consume the apparent difference.

Illustrative assumptions; no live quote or promised outcome.

Try it in your research

Compare the same asset on two venues using the same quote currency and timestamp. Note spread and depth as well as the last traded price.
