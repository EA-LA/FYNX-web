Narrative Rotation Framework

Track sector narratives without chasing every pump.

FYNX learning guide • Educational example •

Define a theme before measuring performance

Narrative rotation describes shifting attention among groups of crypto assets. Group membership is subjective and can change after prices rise. Define the assets and the observation period before calculating returns; otherwise a theme assembled from recent winners exaggerates its historical success.

Inspect liquidity and shared dependencies

Assets associated with a theme can depend on the same exchange, collateral asset or funding conditions. Attention does not establish cash flow, adoption or durable value. Compare market structure, token supply events and executable liquidity alongside the story. A paper watchlist should record why an asset was included before observing its subsequent return.

WORKED EXAMPLE

Put the idea into numbers

A five-token basket that excludes a failed token after the fact shows a better return than the basket a trader could actually have held. That is survivorship bias, even if the theme sounds convincing.

Illustrative assumptions; no live quote or promised outcome.

Try it in your research

Create a dated hypothetical theme list with fixed inclusion rules. Record delistings and losing assets as well as winners when reviewing it later.
