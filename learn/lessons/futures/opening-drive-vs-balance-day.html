Opening Drive vs Balance Day

Session classification for better trade selection.

FYNX learning guide • Educational example •

Use session labels as hypotheses

An opening drive describes sustained directional movement near a session open. A balance day describes more two-sided trading within a range. These are observations that develop over time, not facts known at the first tick. Define the opening interval and the evidence needed before applying either label.

Record invalidation and selection bias

An early breakout can reverse, while a quiet opening can precede a trend. For research, record every qualifying session rather than only memorable examples. Distinguish the cash-market open from the futures session boundary, and note scheduled announcements. Comparing different opening definitions without controlling them can produce misleading conclusions.

WORKED EXAMPLE

Put the idea into numbers

Price moves above the first 15-minute range and then closes back inside it. That observation weakens a simple breakout interpretation, but it does not by itself prove that the rest of the day will remain balanced.
