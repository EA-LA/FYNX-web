Separate local time from session time

Forex is decentralized, and session labels describe broad regional activity rather than one universal exchange schedule. London and New York business hours overlap, but their daylight-saving changes occur on different dates. A fixed UTC schedule can therefore be wrong for part of the year.

Observe rather than assume a pattern

Liquidity and realized movement can vary around scheduled releases, holidays and market openings. Compare the same pair over the same observation window before concluding that a session is consistently more active. An overlap does not ensure direction or profitability. Spreads, transaction costs and the time required to manage a position belong in the study.

WORKED EXAMPLE

Put the idea into numbers

A release scheduled for 08:30 in New York retains its local release time while its UTC equivalent changes with daylight saving. Your own local time may change on a different date.
