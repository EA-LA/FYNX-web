Understanding Currency Pair Behavior

Majors, minors, and why spread quality matters.

FYNX learning guide • Educational example •

Read the quote in the correct direction

A currency pair expresses the price of a base currency in units of a quote currency. EUR/USD at 1.10 means one euro is quoted at 1.10 US dollars. Buying the pair creates long exposure to the base and short exposure to the quote currency. Account-currency conversion is a separate step.

Compare execution conditions

Pairs differ in typical spread, liquidity, session activity and sensitivity to announcements. A tight displayed spread can widen around a release or rollover. Contract size and pip conventions also differ, so verify the broker specification before translating a price move into money. “Major” or “minor” is a classification, not a promise of safe execution.

WORKED EXAMPLE

Put the idea into numbers

For 10,000 EUR in EUR/USD, a move from 1.1000 to 1.1010 changes the quote-currency value by $10. That is ten conventional 0.0001 pips before fees.
