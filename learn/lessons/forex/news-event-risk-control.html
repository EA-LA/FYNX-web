News-Event Risk Control

How to trade around CPI/NFP without random exposure.

FYNX learning guide • Educational example •

Identify the release and its baseline

Economic announcements can change expected interest rates and currency valuations, but reactions depend on expectations and positioning. Check the exact event, reference period, timezone and possible revisions. A calendar’s impact label is a summary, not a measurement of the move that will occur.

Plan execution uncertainty explicitly

During a release, available liquidity can shrink and spreads can widen. A stop can fill beyond its trigger, and an initial move can reverse when additional details are read. A paper plan can compare staying flat, reducing exposure or waiting for conditions to settle; none should be described as a guaranteed way to profit from the announcement.

WORKED EXAMPLE

Put the idea into numbers

A jobs headline exceeds the published consensus while the prior month is revised lower. Looking only at the new headline leaves out information that may matter to the market response.

Illustrative assumptions; no live quote or promised outcome.

Try it in your research

Open an official release and list the headline, revision and next scheduled publication. Write a no-trade condition for a hypothetical plan, such as an unacceptable spread.
