Options Contract Basics

Calls, puts, strike selection, and expiration timing.

FYNX learning guide • Educational example •

Read the contract terms

A call gives its holder a contractual right to buy; a put gives a right to sell, subject to the product’s terms. Strike, expiration, exercise style, settlement and multiplier all matter. Standard US equity contracts commonly reference 100 shares, but adjusted and other contracts can differ.

Separate rights from obligations

A buyer pays a premium for the right. A writer accepts obligations and may face assignment under the contract’s rules. An option’s market value before expiration includes more than its immediate exercise value. An expiration payoff calculation therefore does not forecast what the option will trade for tomorrow.

Put the idea into numbers
