Portfolio-Level Options Risk

Manage net delta, gamma, and event risk across positions.

Aggregate with units and direction intact

Portfolio options risk combines quantity, contract multiplier and long or short sign. Net delta is one useful measure, but exposures to different underlyings are not directly comparable without a common risk model. A delta-neutral snapshot can still retain gamma, volatility, liquidity and event risk.

Stress changes beyond a small move

Evaluate several underlying moves, volatility changes and time steps. Pay attention to concentrated expirations, correlated underlyings and short positions that can require margin or delivery. The Greeks change as markets move, and offsets that look effective in a small local estimate may weaken during a large shock.

WORKED EXAMPLE

Put the idea into numbers

Two long calls at 0.40 delta and a 100 multiplier represent approximately +80 share-equivalent delta. Another position can offset that delta without offsetting the same gamma or vega.
