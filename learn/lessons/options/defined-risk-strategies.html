Understand the combined position

A vertical spread combines long and short options of the same type and expiration at different strikes. The combined expiration payoff can have a bounded gain and loss when the legs remain intact. That payoff definition does not remove execution, early assignment or expiration-management risk.

Check the cash flows and leg handling

A debit spread pays a net premium; a credit spread receives one. Include contract multipliers and fees, and inspect what happens if only one leg is filled or assigned. Closing one leg changes the risk. Product exercise and settlement rules determine whether delivery or cash obligations can arise.
