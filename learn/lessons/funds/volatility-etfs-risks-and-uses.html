Volatility ETFs: Risks and Uses

When VIX-linked products are useful—and when to avoid them.

FYNX learning guide • Educational example •
Identify the actual instrumentUnderstand the path of returnsWorked examplePractice

Identify the actual instrument

A volatility-linked product may hold futures or use derivatives rather than own the spot volatility index. Its objective can be daily, leveraged or inverse. Read the prospectus to identify the benchmark, reset period and whether the product is a fund or an unsecured exchange-traded note.

Understand the path of returns

Rolling futures changes exposure as contracts approach expiration. The shape of the futures curve can create a return drag or benefit that differs from changes in the spot index. Daily leverage also means multi-day returns depend on the path, not just the starting and ending level. These features make a volatility product very different from a simple long-term hedge.
