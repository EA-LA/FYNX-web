Framework for pre-earnings preparation and post-print execution.

Separate the announcement from the reaction

An earnings release can contain revenue, profit, guidance and revisions to earlier expectations. A headline beat does not determine the next price move. Compare the reported period, accounting basis and outlook with what the market was expecting, then observe how price and liquidity actually respond.

Define scenarios before the release

For a paper exercise, distinguish a gap that holds above the prior range from one that returns inside it. Write the condition that would invalidate each interpretation. If a position spans the announcement, an opening gap may skip a stop entirely. Options add another layer because implied volatility can fall after an event even when the stock moves in the anticipated direction.

WORKED EXAMPLE

Put the idea into numbers

A stock closes at 100, opens at 108 after earnings and later trades at 103. It is up on the day, but a trader who bought at the opening price is losing before costs.

Illustrative assumptions; no live quote or promised outcome.

Try it in your research

Read one company release from its investor-relations website. List three facts separately from three interpretations. Compare the opening move with the closing move without assuming a repeatable strategy.
