Event-Day Positioning

How to adjust around FOMC and high-impact releases.

FYNX learning guide • Educational example •

Separate the decision from expectations

Index markets can react to policy statements, forecasts and press conferences as well as a headline rate decision. An unchanged rate can still produce a large move if expectations change. Identify the release sequence and official timezone before studying an event day.

Plan several observable outcomes

Write hypotheses for stronger, weaker or mixed information, then describe how each could be contradicted. The first price move may reverse during later commentary. Order-book depth and spreads can change suddenly. A paper plan should state acceptable execution conditions and whether it assumes holding through the event or waiting afterward.

WORKED EXAMPLE

Put the idea into numbers

A policy rate is unchanged, but the accompanying projections imply a different future path. The market response may reflect the new path rather than the unchanged current rate.
