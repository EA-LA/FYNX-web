How Smart Bias is calculated

Four parts, each scored from −100 to +100, then averaged with fixed weights. If a part has no current data it is left out and the other weights are scaled up; the coverage is shown on every row.

  • Trend (35%): where the price sits inside its 20-day high–low range, scaled by how cleanly it has trended (efficiency ratio). Uses the live delayed quote when available.
  • Yield momentum (30%): one-month change in the 2-year yield of each currency's government bonds (5-year for the UK). A widening gap in favor of the first currency scores positive.
  • Rate gap (20%): difference between the two central bank policy rates. A 3-point gap or more scores ±100.
  • Positioning (15%): CFTC speculator positioning. Only when it is crowded does it score, and then against the crowd.

This is a summary of public data, not a forecast or a trade recommendation.
