01 / WHERE IT BEGAN

A simple reason.
A bigger purpose.

FYNX began with the retail trader in mind: someone making their own decisions, managing their own risk, and trying to find a clearer way forward.

Founded by Elham Amini, FYNX brings practical trading tools and education into the same world. A calculation should connect to an explanation. Market research should connect to a plan. A trading journal should make it easier to learn from the decisions that came before.

That is the idea behind Finance World: give independent traders a useful place to do the work around a trade. Learn the concept, understand the numbers, consider the risk, and return to the process.

From that starting point, the ambition has grown into a wider FYNX ecosystem. The purpose stays close to the original idea: build things that help retail traders.

04 / WHERE WE WANT TO GO

Build the foundation.
Then build further.

The long-term FYNX plan moves through three layers: trading tools, a connected financial ecosystem, and institutional capabilities.

This is our strategic direction. These ambitions are not a list of completed features or promised release dates. The product pages describe what is available today.

01THE FOUNDATIONA better trading workspaceStart with the tools and context around a trader’s daily decisions.

The first layer centers on the dashboard, trading tools, charts, market news, and performance systems. The roadmap also explores execution and broker infrastructure as part of the wider foundation.

  • Core dashboard
  • Entry trading
  • Trading charts
  • Broker infrastructure
  • Funded platform
  • Market news hub
02THE CONNECTIONSA wider financial ecosystemBring intelligence and new financial capabilities into the same vision.

The next layer explores digital assets, AI-assisted tools, and financial services beyond the trading workspace. It also includes the long-term ambition of taking FYNX to the public markets.

  • Crypto & wallet
  • FYNX AI
  • FYNX public company
  • FYNX coin
  • Banking services

These are future ambitions, subject to development and applicable requirements.

03THE LONG VIEWBuild toward institutional scaleExtend the ambition into capital, businesses, and financial infrastructure.

The longer horizon looks at investment capabilities, private equity, investment banking, and products that could serve enterprises and institutions. It is the broadest expression of the FYNX vision.

  • Private equity
  • Investment banking
  • Wall Street scale

Long-term vision; these are not current service offerings.
