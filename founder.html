01 / THE STARTING POINT

It begins with
the retail trader.

Elham Amini started FYNX to help retail traders. That purpose is the clearest introduction to the person behind the brand, and to the kind of financial products FYNX is being built to become.

As an entrepreneur and product builder, Elham’s work brings together trading tools, market education, and systems for understanding performance. The emphasis is on the practical work around a decision: learning what an instrument does, making sense of a calculation, considering exposure, and reviewing the result.

For someone trading independently, those pieces often need to come together through their own effort. FYNX is built around making that process easier to navigate, with tools and explanations that have a clear purpose in a trader’s routine.

The mission is personal in its origin and practical in its expression. Helping retail traders starts with giving them something useful to work with.

02 / THE APPROACH

Clarity in the product.
Discipline in the process.

Precision, independence, and long-term thinking sit at the center of Elham’s approach to FYNX. Each has a practical meaning for the product.

Precision means making the numbers and their assumptions easier to understand. A position-size calculator, for example, gives a trader a way to connect an account balance, a stop distance, and a chosen risk amount. Its value is in helping someone think through a decision.

Independence means keeping the trader’s own judgment at the center. Education and tools can support that judgment. They can help a person ask better questions, understand limitations, and form a process they can explain to themselves.

Long-term thinking means connecting the work. A useful calculator can lead into a lesson. Research can inform a plan. A journal can turn a completed trade into something worth reviewing. Together, those steps give the product a direction beyond any single feature.

03 / ONE CONNECTED IDEA

Building the world
around the tools.

FYNX Finance World is the public home for calculators, learning resources, market research, and a trading journal. The mobile app carries the product into another part of a trader’s day. FYNX Funded has its own focus on evaluations and program requirements, while the API opens a developer-facing side of the ecosystem.

These products express different parts of the same ambition: create a financial technology brand with depth, where the pieces have a reason to belong together.

For Elham, that wider direction starts with the original audience. The usefulness of the work comes back to the retail trader: what they need to understand, what they need to calculate, and how they can better organize their own decisions.

04 / THE LONG VIEW

A purpose to keep.
A vision to grow into.

The long-term vision for FYNX extends into a broader financial ecosystem, with ambitions across analytics, infrastructure, capital, and institutional capabilities. The public roadmap gives that direction a place to be explored.

Those ambitions describe the horizon. The work begins with the foundation: useful products, understandable tools, and a coherent experience for the people using them.

Elham’s reason for starting FYNX remains the point of reference as that vision develops. Help retail traders. Give the tools a purpose. Build something that can continue to grow.
