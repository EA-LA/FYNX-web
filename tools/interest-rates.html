Central bank rates

Compare the latest published policy-rate observations from the Bank for International Settlements. Check the observation date before using a value.

Daily observations are published by BIS weekly. Policy instruments vary by central bank; use each card’s series definition. A retrieval timestamp is not the date of a policy decision.

Source and updates

The source is checked automatically when the six-hour server cache expires. If a refresh fails, any retained observations are labeled with their original retrieval time. No estimates are substituted for missing data.

Assumptions and limitations

A policy rate is not the interest paid on every deposit or loan. Stale observations should not be presented as current decisions.
