How research watchlists works

Build a list with a research purpose

Enter one to thirty symbols separated by commas and write the question that connects them. The tool normalizes symbols to uppercase and removes repeated entries. Notes can describe an upcoming event, an observation to verify or the reason a symbol belongs on the list. A ticker alone may be ambiguous across exchanges, so record the venue or instrument context where necessary.

Separate organization from market data

The result contains the saved symbol list, notes and links into FYNX Symbol News. It does not stream prices, verify that a ticker exists on every venue or place trades. Save a named workspace to retain the list across signed-in sessions. Revisit the notes after a scheduled release and distinguish what changed from what was already known.

Assumptions and limits

A watchlist is an organizational aid. Prices, alert delivery and trading signals are not supplied. News coverage depends on the connected provider and the symbol query.
