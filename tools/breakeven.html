HOW TO USE THIS TOOL

Account for the costs of entering and exiting

Start with your inputs

Enter the direction, size, entry and the fees supported by this calculator. Keep percentage fees and fixed dollar charges distinct.

The calculation or concept

Break-even is the exit price where net proceeds equal the entry cost plus modeled trading costs.

Worked example

For 100 shares bought at $50 with $10 in total fixed costs, the long trade needs a $50.10 exit to break even, assuming no additional spread or slippage.

Hypothetical example, not a current quote.
Assumptions and limitations

Borrow charges, funding, tax and changing spreads may be outside the model. A displayed break-even level only includes the costs you enter.
