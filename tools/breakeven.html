HOW TO USE THIS TOOL

Account for the costs of entering and exiting

Start with your inputs

Enter the direction, entry, lot size, spread and commission. Choose a 0.01 pip size for JPY pairs and enter the pip value per lot for your pair ($10 for pairs quoted in USD). The table under the result shows the net result after costs for example moves.

The calculation or concept

Break-even is the exit price where net proceeds equal the entry cost plus modeled trading costs.

Worked example

Buying 1 lot of EURUSD at 1.10000 with a 1-pip spread and $7 round-trip commission costs $17, or 1.7 pips at $10 a pip, so the trade breaks even at 1.10017. A 10-pip move in your favour then nets $83.

Hypothetical example, not a current quote.
Assumptions and limitations

Borrow charges, funding, tax and changing spreads may be outside the model. A displayed break-even level only includes the costs you enter.
