How scenario comparison works

Keep the trade assumption fixed

Enter the same balance, price levels, multiplier and conversion rate used by the position risk planner. This tool evaluates four risk budgets: one-half, one, one-and-a-half and two times the entered risk percentage. The model caps each scenario at 100% of balance; that computational boundary is not a recommended allocation.

Compare exposure rather than just the upside

Each row shows risk budget, calculated units, loss at the stop, profit at the target and reward-to-risk ratio. With fixed price levels, increasing size scales both loss and profit, while the price-based ratio remains the same. Use the rows to understand the trade-off before recording your own decision. This is a deterministic comparison of entered assumptions, not a probability-weighted outcome model.

ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE

Follow the inputs through.

With $10,000 of balance, 1% base risk and a two-unit stop distance at a multiplier and conversion rate of 1, the four risk budgets are $50, $100, $150 and $200. They correspond to 25, 50, 75 and 100 units before broker rounding.

Assumptions and limits

The comparison excludes fees, slippage, broker lot increments and margin rules. It does not make a larger risk allocation preferable or guarantee that a stop will contain a loss.
