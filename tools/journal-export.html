How journal export works

Know what the export contains

The export includes date, symbol, session, realized P/L and notes from up to 1,000 dated trades returned by your account workspace. It is a Journal-data export, not a broker statement. Entry prices, stops and targets are not columns in this export. The download displays its row count so you can compare it with the period and records you expected.

Check the spreadsheet interpretation

Open the CSV with a spreadsheet import flow and confirm how dates and decimal values are interpreted in your locale. Quoted text preserves commas and quotation marks inside notes. Text beginning with certain spreadsheet formula characters is escaped for safer opening. Keep the original export before applying filters, sorting or editing values in a working copy. The export operation does not alter your saved Journal.

Assumptions and limits

An export is only as complete and accurate as the recorded trades and returned sample. It is not a tax report or proof of broker execution. Store downloaded files appropriately if they contain private notes.
