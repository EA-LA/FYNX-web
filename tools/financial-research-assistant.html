How this simulation works

Compare fast and slow simple moving averages of completed daily closes. When fast is above slow, buy at the next session’s open; otherwise, exit at the next open. The model uses all available cash and fractional units. It liquidates at the final close. Slippage and fixed commissions apply on each side, including the buy-and-hold comparison.

Downloaded OHLC values are scaled by the provider’s adjusted close. Dividends are not added again. The current exchange day is excluded. Results are hypothetical, omit taxes and interest, and do not model order-book liquidity, intraday drawdowns or trading restrictions. A backtest is not a prediction.

07 / PRICE ALERTS

Catch a change that matters.

One-time crossing alerts for prices and percentage changes.

Keep this research page open and visible.

Checks run about once a minute while this page is visible. No closed-tab, background or server alerts. The first fresh quote sets a baseline; an alert fires only on a later crossing.

In-page alerts are always available.
Alert timing and limits

Only quotes no more than 15 minutes old can establish a baseline or trigger an alert. Repeated timestamps are ignored. An above crossing means the previous value was below your threshold and the new value is at or above it; below works in reverse. Rearm a fired alert to begin with a new baseline. Delivery depends on your browser, notification permission, network and public data source.

How to use this tool

Select a company in Company financials to prepare a brief grounded in its reported facts. The standard brief works without a paid AI account. Optional on-device AI depends on browser support and may require a model download. Verify the figures and the original filing before relying on a summary.
