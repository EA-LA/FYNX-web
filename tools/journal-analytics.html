How journal intelligence works

Read the sample before the statistics

The analysis uses up to 1,000 dated Journal trades from your account. It reports the trade count, net realized profit or loss, win rate, average result per trade, profit factor and maximum realized drawdown. Record the actual closed-trade result in one account currency. A missing fee or an omitted losing trade changes the interpretation of every summary.

Interpret each number separately

Expectancy here is average recorded P/L per trade, not a forecast. Profit factor divides gross winning P/L by the absolute total of losing P/L; it is undefined when there are no recorded losses. Drawdown measures a fall from a peak in cumulative realized P/L. It does not include unrealized losses, deposits or a starting account balance. Compare the statistics with your notes and the period covered before drawing conclusions.

Assumptions and limits

A small or selectively recorded sample can be misleading. Historical outcomes do not establish future profitability. This tool does not reconstruct intratrade risk or connect to your broker automatically.
