Pivot Calculator

Calculate classic pivot points using the previous period’s high, low, and close. Get the main pivot, resistance levels, and support levels instantly for forex, crypto, stocks, or indices.

INPUTS

Enter the previous candle or session values. Choose the pivot method and calculate support and resistance levels.

RESULT

Enter values and tap Calculate
Your pivot levels will appear here.
Formula uses previous session values. Classic gives PP, R1, R2, R3, S1, S2, S3. Fibonacci gives PP with fib-based support and resistance levels. Woodie weights the close twice: PP = (H + L + 2C) ÷ 4. Camarilla places R1–R4 and S1–S4 around the close at 1.1 × range ÷ 12, 6, 4 and 2. Add a current price (optional) to see the nearest level above and below it; the table compares all four methods for the same candle.
HOW TO USE THIS TOOL

Calculate reference levels from the previous period

Start with your inputs

Enter high, low and close from the same completed session and check the selected method. Confirm your data provider’s session boundary.

The calculation or concept

The standard pivot is (high + low + close) ÷ 3. Fibonacci spaces levels at 38.2%, 61.8% and 100% of the range; Woodie gives the close double weight; Camarilla sets four tighter levels each side of the close.

Worked example

A previous high of 110, low of 100 and close of 105 gives a standard pivot of 105. Standard R1 is 110 and S1 is 100.

Hypothetical example, not a current quote.
Assumptions and limitations

Different sessions and pivot methods produce different levels. A pivot is a reference level, not a forecast or automatic order.
