Retracements measure a pullback from the end of the selected swing. Extensions here project from the beginning of that same swing; this is a two-point calculation, not a three-point projection. The 50% midpoint is included by convention. Levels are reference prices, not predictions or trade recommendations.

HOW TO USE THIS TOOL

Map retracement levels from a defined swing

Start with your inputs

Choose a completed swing high and low, set the direction, and compare the generated levels with other market structure.

The calculation or concept

For an upward swing, retracement price = swing high − (swing high − swing low) × retracement fraction.

Worked example

A move from 100 to 120 has a range of 20. A 50% retracement is 110; a 61.8% retracement is 107.64.

Hypothetical example, not a current quote.
Assumptions and limitations

Changing the swing changes every level. Fibonacci levels are reference points, not evidence that price must reverse there.
