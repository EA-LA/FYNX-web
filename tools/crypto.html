HOW TO USE THIS TOOL

Estimate a crypto trade using explicit cost assumptions

Start with your inputs

Enter asset quantity, entry and exit assumptions, and the applicable fees. Confirm whether your plan concerns spot holdings or a leveraged derivative.

The calculation or concept

Gross P/L for a simple long spot position = quantity × (exit price − entry price). Net P/L subtracts modeled costs.

Worked example

Buying 0.1 units at $30,000 and selling at $31,000 produces $100 gross profit. Fees on both transactions reduce the net result.

Hypothetical example, not a current quote.
Assumptions and limitations

Spot arithmetic does not fully model perpetual funding, exchange margin tiers or liquidation. Quotes and liquidity differ between venues.
