HOW TO USE THIS TOOL

Compare a target with the planned downside

Start with your inputs

Enter entry, stop and target; the calculator detects long or short from which side of entry the stop sits. Add your win rate from past trades to see expectancy and the losing streak to plan for, and use the 1R/2R/3R targets to compare exits without moving your stop simply to improve the ratio.

The calculation or concept

Reward-to-risk ratio = distance to target ÷ distance to stop, for the chosen direction.

Worked example

A long entry at $100 with a $98 stop and $106 target has $2 of price risk and $6 of potential reward per unit: 3:1 before costs.

Hypothetical example, not a current quote.
Assumptions and limitations

A large ratio does not make a setup profitable. Win rate, execution costs and the frequency of reaching the target also matter.
