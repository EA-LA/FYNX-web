HOW TO USE THIS TOOL

Estimate a debt payoff schedule

Start with your inputs

Enter your balance, annual rate and recurring payment. Compare the time and interest under an additional-payment scenario.

The calculation or concept

Each modeled period adds interest and subtracts the payment. Payoff requires the payment to exceed the accrued interest.

Worked example

A $1,000 balance at a nominal 12% annual rate accrues about $10 during the first month under a monthly model. A $100 payment reduces principal by about $90.

Hypothetical example, not a current quote.
Assumptions and limitations

Actual lenders may use daily interest, fees and variable rates, so confirm your lender’s schedule.
