How trading process review works

Record the decision in your own words

Write the trade thesis, the evidence considered and the lesson you want to retain. The workspace accepts up to 2,000 characters of notes. Separate facts available before the trade from interpretations made after the outcome. A profitable result does not prove that the decision process was sound, and a losing result does not by itself prove the opposite.

Review five concrete checks

The checklist covers a defined entry plan, a checked risk budget, scheduled events, execution that followed the plan, and a recorded outcome and lesson. Each checked item contributes 20 points to a completion score. The result lists completed and missing checks so that the next review has a clear starting point. Save a named workspace when you want to retain the notes and checklist state.

Assumptions and limits

This is a self-assessment, not a trading performance rating or an independent audit. The tool does not verify that a checked action occurred. The quality of the notes matters more than the score.
