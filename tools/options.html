HOW TO USE THIS TOOL

Understand an option payoff at expiration

Start with your inputs

Select the contract type and position, then enter strike, premium and contract quantity. Check the tool’s 100-unit contract assumption. Distinguish expiration payoff from today’s market value.

The calculation or concept

Long-call expiration P/L per unit = max(underlying price − strike, 0) − premium. Multiply by the contract multiplier.

Worked example

A call with a 100 strike and a 3 premium breaks even at 103 at expiration before fees. If the underlying ends at 110, profit is 7 per unit, or $700 for a 100-unit contract.

Hypothetical example, not a current quote.
Assumptions and limitations

Before expiration, implied volatility, time value and other factors affect price. Exercise, assignment and settlement can introduce obligations beyond a simple payoff diagram.
