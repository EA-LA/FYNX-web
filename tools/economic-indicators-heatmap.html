Read the dates, not a score

Each value shows its observation year. These annual series provide historical economic context; they are not real-time releases, surprise scores, or trading signals. Reporting years can differ between economies. Unemployment uses the World Bank's modeled ILO estimate. Open the economic calendar for upcoming releases.

Source and updates

The source is checked automatically when the six-hour server cache expires. If a refresh fails, any retained observations are labeled with their original retrieval time. No estimates are substituted for missing data.
